01 / Learn the rules

Asking questions in the simple present

We use the simple present to ask about facts, habits, and regular routines.
Thì hiện tại đơn dùng để hỏi về sự thật, thói quen và hoạt động thường ngày.

Yes / No questions

Do + I / you / we / they + verb (base form) + ?
Does + he / she / it + verb (base form) + ?
Do you like soccer?
Does she work on the weekend?
Do they go out a lot?

Short answers

Yes, I / you / we / they do.
No, I / you / we / they don't.
Yes, he / she / it does.
No, he / she / it doesn't.
Do you work at a café? → Yes, I do.
Does he work from home? → No, he doesn't.
Remember: After does / doesn't, use the base verb. Does she live here? ✅   Does she lives here? ❌
02 / Wh- questions

Ask for more information

Question word + do / does + subject + verb (base form) + ?
Where do you live?
Who do you live with?
What does he do on the weekend?
How do you get to work?
When does the class start?
Why do you like soccer?
Question words: what (cái gì), where (ở đâu), when (khi nào), why (tại sao), who (ai), how (như thế nào).
💡 Compare: “She works in a bank.” → “Does she work in a bank?” → “Where does she work?”

05 / Pair work

Ask, answer, and follow up

Student A asks a question. Student B answers in a complete sentence, then asks a follow-up question. Change roles after each question.

Conversation template

A: Where do you live?
B: I live in Ho Chi Minh City.
A: Do you live with your family?
B: Yes, I do. What about you?
🎤 Speaking goal: Ask at least 6 questions, use both do and does, and give at least 2 follow-up questions.
